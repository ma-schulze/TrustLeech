We evaluate the injection time as 1.2 seconds in the paper, most of this time is spent setting up the virtio drivers.

In the CloudLab environment, we oftentimes observe higher results, approximately 4.4 seconds. 

We believe this being caused by the different hardware platforsm:
Since the TrustLeech-hypervisor's injection requires much interaction with the emulated virtio devices (whose execution speed depends on the host), this introduces deviations.

Nontheless, we plan to further look into what exactly is causing this and whether this can be mitigated.
