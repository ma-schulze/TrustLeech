We evaluate the injection time as 1.2 seconds in the paper, most of this time is spent setting up the virtio drivers.

In the CloudLab environment, we oftentimes observe smaller results. 
We believe this is caused by the environment running on a faster system than the environment used for the paper's evaluation. 
Since the TrustLeech-hypervisor's interaction with the emulated virtio devices executes a lot of QEMU code, 
which is executed at speeds depending on the host hardware, we see different speeds.
